# Using real DMarket funds

Version 1.0 includes this workflow to the local webpage. Real marketplace operations remain manual. The program starts with **no recorded real balance**; the existing $10 paper experiment is separate.

1. Open **Funds**. Record the regular and restricted Tradable amounts DMarket actually shows after your manually handled start route. Use a receipt or a saved balance-record reference. This is the opening balance, not a made-up prediction or profit record for your start route.
2. Use **Routes**, choosing **Real**, then search. The search uses money that is not spent or committed. Once real funds have been recorded, normal background research uses that pool too. You can still select the shared paper experiment manually.
3. Review an estimate, including its selling method, fees, source times and unknowns. Choose **Start real trial** before making the purchase. The original estimate and model stay fixed. This reserves the proposed money; it does not record a purchase or place an order. Listing-price estimates are supported for manual real routes.
4. Buy manually. Select the active route, then open **Record a real operation**, record the actual quantities, amounts paid from each DMarket account, included fees and receipt reference. Partial initial purchases keep the unused planned money reserved until you mark the purchases finished. An unused plan can be cancelled without creating a trading outcome.
5. Record receipt on Steam and the actual unlock time, then actual Steam sales. Partial sales are supported. Each receipt saves its money, items and current step together. When all outward items are sold, record the return purchase and its actual unlock time. The return item may differ from the original prediction within the supported CS2 roster; the original choice is preserved for comparison.
6. After the actual wait, record the transfer back to DMarket and each sale receipt. Choose the account actually credited: regular or Tradable. Record separately any cost paid outside DMarket. For a group of return purchases with different unlock times, the route uses the latest known unlock for the held batch; mixed return item types are not supported by this form.
7. Once everything is settled, open **Update / resolve route** and choose **Resolve route**. Holdings and Steam change must be accounted for. Liquidation or write-off is an explicit user decision; the program never makes it automatically. Only then do results show profit/loss and differences from the original estimate. Resolved recorded outcomes refine later estimates in their own real/paper, item-family and selling-engine group.

Actual receipts remain recordable after the original quote expires or market sources go offline. Recorded unlock times can be corrected using **Stage / unlock time** before a receipt whose date conflicts with them. A missing unlock time stays unknown; it is not invented from a quote. Record the actual operation date, not the predicted date. Timestamps are displayed in your local time.

## Funds, receipts and corrections

- Funding amounts use USD on the page. Enter up to two decimal places. Regular and Tradable funds are shown separately. A restriction release moves money between them and creates no income.
- Opening balance is recorded once. Later outside additions or withdrawals are changes, not new balance snapshots. Record DMarket proceeds from a tracked route in that route, not as another outside deposit. Receipt references prevent recording the same funding as route income.
- The initial plan uses restricted funds first where the existing CS2 market-purchase rules allow it. Actual purchases record the accounts you really used. Account amounts and commitments prevent double use; this is bookkeeping, not a loss limit or open-route valuation.
- Fees are already included in the supplied net receipt or purchase total. They are recorded for comparison and never deducted a second time.
- Use unique references, including the service/operation when necessary, such as `steam-sale:receipt123` and `steam-transfer:trade456`. Retrying the same saved operation cannot charge it twice.
- Funding corrections retain the original entry. Money/item corrections for an open route also retain both records and check that the corrected history does not spend money or sell items before receipt. For receipts saved by the real-operation form, a correction cannot turn a purchase into a sale or vice versa. Record a separate refund or exceptional operation using the general update form.
- A closed result stays frozen. The existing linked-recovery form remains available for assets previously written off. No automated loss limit or one-month stop has been added.

Automatic paper fills for listing scenarios remain unsupported: an asking price is not evidence of a sale. That limit does not prevent opening a manual real route from a listing estimate and recording its actual receipts. Automatic independent comparison trials remain planned for v1.1.
