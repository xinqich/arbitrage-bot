# Development plan

Updated 2026-09-16. This replaces earlier desktop-window and calendar-bound plans.

The goal is reliable net growth, starting with $10, with eventual withdrawal through CSFloat. There is no profit target, fixed end date, automatic loss limit, or open-route profit/loss. Evidence collection continues while routes wait. Only resolved routes produce outcomes and train later predictions. The user manages risk and performs real Steam/market actions.

## Route purposes

- Start: handled manually by the user. CSFloat integration is postponed. The earlier separate pretend $10 CSFloat balance stays dormant and does not fund DMarket routes.
- Grow: DMarket -> Steam -> DMarket. Choose items and whole-item quantities again each cycle, using several price levels and current remaining funds.
- Withdraw (postponed until requested): compare direct transfer of DMarket items through Steam inventory to CSFloat with selling and buying again on the Steam Market before CSFloat. Include selling/payout fees, minimums, conversion costs and waits. CSFloat access does not promise a KYC-free withdrawal.

## Corrected planning rule: opportunities are not proven outcomes

The error was treating "credible selling assumptions" as if each candidate had to demonstrate future liquidity or successful historical cycles before being suggested. The approved-design sentence "No profitable-route claim until complete economics and delays are backed by relevant outcomes" wrongly applied an economic-proof requirement to opportunity discovery. This section supersedes that interpretation and any ambiguous blanket reference to "insufficient evidence" elsewhere in the plan.

**v1.0 must actively suggest potentially profitable routes before there is enough history to establish consistent results.** M7 measures actual reliability later. Do not turn M7, a minimum learning period, daily sales coverage, a sale-count threshold, or completed-trial count into a hidden prerequisite for discovery or release.

| Situation | Planned behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Priceable positive-net route, but little/no sales history or no completed outcomes | Keep it among the opportunities. Show that demand persistence, sale time and predictive accuracy are unknown. Do not reject it, apply an invented penalty that makes its return negative, or silently hide it behind a confidence cutoff. |
| No guarantee that today's bids will still exist after the wait | Show the conditional return at those prices and the known minimum waits. Future price/demand is an assumption for every route, not a reason to discard all routes. |
| A possible exit relies on listing an item rather than filling a current buy order | Keep a separately labelled listing-price scenario when an actual price source supports it. State that its sale price/time/fill are assumptions. An asking price is not a completed sale or a guaranteed receipt. |
| Observed order depth cannot fill the proposed quantity | Limit the instant-fill scenario to supported quantities or show its partial fill. A listing/waiting alternative may remain a candidate with clear assumptions. Do not infer that the item can never sell. |
| Missing/old essential price, unknown item identity or unusable source format | Explain which calculation cannot currently be made and retain the item/route as needing data or refresh. Do not invent prices/profit and do not label missing data as proven illiquidity. A clearly labelled last-observed scenario can remain visible; it is not a current executable quote. |
| Known incompatible account/item restriction, unaffordable purchase, or nonpositive net under the stated scenario | Explain the concrete problem with that scenario. A known lock is a modeled wait when the route supports waiting, not automatically a permanent rejection. |

Source qualification means checking what a field represents (price, quantity, identity, timestamp), not requiring proof that an item has sufficient completed sales. Confidence/uncertainty must be explained in plain words; do not invent numerical success probabilities before calibration. Ranking must make expected economics and assumptions inspectable rather than make absent history a disguised exclusion rule.

Separate discovery from simulated or real execution. A suggested future sale may use a stated assumption; recording a paper fill still needs an eligible, sufficiently fresh observation and supported quantity. Real fills need the user's actual transaction records. Never fabricate sales to make a trial advance. Existing frozen trial rules/history are not changed by this planning correction.

Before implementing v1.0 route selection, audit the current rejection reasons and test: (1) positive-net candidates survive without history; (2) unknown completion time stays unknown without rejecting the route; (3) missing prices are distinct from observed absence of demand; (4) insufficient instant-fill depth does not erase a separately priced listing alternative; (5) known restrictions, affordability and fee arithmetic still apply. Record any runtime gaps as implementation work rather than claiming this documentation update already fixes the code.

### v1.1 - M8 automatic comparison trials

Approved for later implementation; no scheduling or budget behavior changes merely from this plan update.

1. Run route selection alongside the normal six-hour research schedule. Choose the best 5-10 **distinct** supported routes, with fewer or none when distinct priceable positive-net scenarios are unavailable or known execution constraints rule them out. Missing history or unproven future demand alone must not reduce the selection to zero. Do not pad the set with small quantity variations of the same route.
2. Start with a $10 reference budget. Once the user supplies updated real funds, use their current DMarket budget. While the user is in a real route, keep using the balance saved immediately before that route's entry. Record the reference amount, balance types, source and time for every comparison; do not substitute the reduced in-route balance, the existing trial's unused 65 cents, or a prediction of unfinished holdings' value.
3. Each trial is an alternative use of the **same** reference budget. Ten $10 alternatives are ten choices, not $100 available or ten simultaneous investments. Give alternatives separate simulated money and depth state; enforce no double use within each alternative. The same observed offer may appear in mutually exclusive alternatives. Actual account commitments and existing shared-budget paper trials retain their current restrictions and history.
4. Automatically save each trial's original prediction/model/evidence and rules, then follow fresh observations after eligibility. Keep existing freshness, identity, fees, costs, quantity and residual rules. Record a new simulation version/mode for independent alternatives rather than silently changing older trials.
5. Avoid opening the same comparison again every scan; define duplicate/cooldown rules and a bounded active-trial limit. Collect shared evidence once where possible, stay within source/total allowances, and preserve scheduling and consumed commands across restart.
6. Show the reference budget, comparison group, selection reason and waiting state in the page, with controls to enable/pause automatic trial creation and inspect individual trials. Starting/stopping selection must not erase existing trials.
7. Compare predictions with outcomes only after resolution. Never add alternative profits together as account growth. Keep outcomes separate from real trades and older paper engines; preserve their shared-observation/comparison origin so learning does not treat repeated or correlated trials as independent proof of demand.

Completion checks: correct $10/current/pre-entry budget selection; fully separate alternatives; no duplicate entries or multiplied apparent capital; fewer-than-five/no-route cases; no consumption of real money; bounded requests; restart/pause behavior; unchanged original trials; final-only evaluation and correct learning partitions. These checks belong to v1.1, not the v1.0 release gate.

## Operation and safeguards

Serve only 127.0.0.1 (default port 8765). No hosting service or external UI account. One worker continues when the tab closes. Restart after Windows sign-in unless the user deliberately paused. Six-hour collection, with checks at paper eligibility times, shared persistent request allowances, bounded transient retries and clear authentication/format/quota blockers. Open routes get priority within the same cycle and provider allowances. A targeted check does not postpone the six-hour research scan. A failed source stops only its own requests; the paper engine still requires valid evidence for the current step and every title in its frozen return basket. Missed time is a gap, never reconstructed as pretend live coverage.

Preserve the original route prediction/model. Every paper decision records time, engine, observed levels and source IDs. Aggregate depth is shared across paper routes and is replenished only by visible additional volume; disappearance is not a confirmed sale. Wait for user instructions about Steam cents left over. Never automatically write off or liquidate them.

A local session token and matching Origin protect POST actions; paths are allowlisted and credentials stay in Python. The page refreshes every five seconds and shows disconnection. Account restrictions remain attached to funds: DMarket Tradable counts in growth outcomes but cannot fund unsupported actions.

Later games: Dota 2, Rust and Team Fortress 2, including cross-game routes, after each game's fees, identity, delays, evidence and balance permissions are qualified. No CS2 delay assumption is silently reused.
